PAGE 07 / 07 TREATMENT PROTOCOLS // COUNTERMEASURES
SCHEDULED ACTIONS

Daily treatments to prevent vision changes, bone loss, and radiation damage:

EYE & HEAD PRESSURE

LBNP & Braslet Cuffs

Why: Microgravity causes cephalad (headward) fluid shifts, increasing intraocular pressure (SANS). LBNP and thigh cuffs pull fluids back to the lower extremities.

Use: 45 min at -25 mmHg LBNP before sleep.
BONE STRENGTH

ARED & CEVIS Exercise

Why: Weight-bearing bones lose 1-1.5% calcium per month in zero-G. High-load resistive exercise (ARED) and cardiovascular cycling (CEVIS) stimulate bone remodeling.

Use: 2.5 hrs/day total (e.g., 4x8 squats).
RADIATION DEFENSE

Dosimetry & SPE Shielding

Why: Deep space exposes crew to Galactic Cosmic Rays (GCRs) and Solar Particle Events (SPEs). Tissue Equivalent Proportional Counters (TEPC) monitor real-time dose.

Use: Move to water-shielded core if dose spikes.
